# Aegis spell bar

`Assets/Scripts/Presentation/SciFiActionBar.cs` draws the bottom HUD using transparent textures from `Assets/Resources/UI/Aegis`.

- Eight equal square slots (60 × 60 HUD units), tightly spaced, with small keycaps underneath. The ultimate uses the same shape with a crimson accent.
- Icons are selected by ability type, including the eight Vanguard abilities and eight Bounty Hunter abilities.
- Cyan: ready. Crimson: cooldown. Muted: insufficient energy. White: currently casting/channeling. Overcharge adds a pulsing golden outer halo without recoloring the icon or slot interior.
- Only icons, ability-specific cooldown numbers and keys appear on the slots. The global cooldown uses a visual wipe without a number; its progress is normalized separately from the spell cooldown. Readiness, costs and proc names remain in tooltips or visual states.
- Health and energy are stacked inside one illuminated frame on the left above the slots, showing current / maximum values and percentages. Hover health for additional shield values. The target uses the same row positions and always the same frame height as the player: red HP above its optional resource. Its name labels the HP row and appears in full on hover. Targets without an Energy component (or with no resource capacity) leave the lower row empty; targets with Energy use its live values and configurable HUD name/color. The frame disappears without a living, active hostile target. Target shields and debuffs are available on hover.
- The compact 520 × 152 bar follows the existing 900-unit HUD canvas and scales down on narrow screens. The additional resource row extends upwards; the square slots keep their previous size and bottom position. The existing keyboard controls activate spells; hovering shows descriptions.
- Both cast bars share a thin frame and a full-width yellow progress field. Spell name and elapsed / total seconds overlay the fill, with outlined text for contrast. The fill runs all the way to the frame's right inner edge at 100%, including behind the time, using the gameplay cast/channel timer. The overhead bar (100 × 17) matches the HP frame width and touches its bottom edge. It scales down the typography and shortens long spell names. The lower bar remains 420 × 24. Casting and channeling use the same yellow.
- Overhead player HP is a small light-green bar (100 × 14) with a one-unit border and only its percentage centered inside. It has no absolute values, title or large metallic decoration.
- The overhead cast bar takes its position and width directly from the HP rectangle, with no gap; the main cast bar is anchored eight HUD units above the action-bar resource panels and uses the same scale. Tooltips draw over it when hovered.
- A slim boss HP bar sits at the top center, using subdued metal trim and a red fill, with its name above and small phase/percentage labels below. The standalone attack text is removed. A yellow cast bar appears underneath only during actual windups or bombardment channeling, with the spell name and elapsed/total seconds inside. BossBrain exposes the live cast state and clears it on interruption, death and disable; recovery and repositioning show no cast bar. The boss HP follows encounter engagement independently of player targeting, hiding before combat and after the encounter ends.
- One compact party frame (208 × 64) sits near the bottom-left corner. It shows UI-only dummy member Nova with 840/1000 HP and 72/100 energy, plus a complementary class emblem. It never duplicates the local player or creates a combat actor. On narrow screens it moves just above the bottom HUD to avoid overlap.
- The damage meter uses the same metal frame with separate total-damage and DPS columns backed by the existing combat statistics. The former top-left title and mission text are removed.
- The top-right minimap uses the Aegis frame and the authored level layout. It follows the player with north up, shows live hostile/target/boss markers, the player heading, cover and gate status, and retains the mission clock. The boss marker stays on the map edge when outside the visible range.
- Enemy overhead HP bars use small framed red fills and percentages. Player overhead HP retains its light-green treatment.
- The target indicator is a ground-aligned red disc with a stronger translucent fill, a broad opaque rim, a bright red highlight and a thin dark outer edge for contrast on light floors. Its radius extends 0.38 units beyond the target collider (minimum 0.7) and sits 0.06 units above the ground. Its mesh and material are owned and cleaned up on deselection, death and disable. The shader is `Assets/Resources/TargetSelection.shader`.

Editable vector sources are in `Assets/Art/UI/Aegis/Source`. The textures have transparent backgrounds, no mipmaps, no compression, bilinear filtering and clamped edges.

To regenerate the assets, run `node Tools/GenerateAegisUi.cjs` with the `sharp` package available (locally installed or via `NODE_PATH`). The generator owns the SVG geometry and preserves existing Unity GUIDs. If changing shapes, update the generator as well as any exported files.

The supplied concept image is a style reference; it is not a runtime dependency. No image-generation service is needed to rebuild these vector assets.
